# Reference data used to build covariates

Small lookup tables from outside SCALES and the FJC case file. `r/01_build_features.R` uses them. They were built on 15 Oct 2026, and the judge table rebuilt on 24 Oct 2026, by `build_reference.py` (in this folder) from the sources below. Pass `-` for a source to skip rebuilding that table.

| File | What it is | Source | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fjc_district_judges.csv` | One row per Article III district-court judgeship active at any point in **2016–2021**: court, judge name, commission, **senior status** and termination dates. 1,279 district judgeships in 91 courts, plus 359 appeals-court and international-trade judgeships (`scales_court` = `appeals` / `intl_trade`), used only to match judges sitting by designation in a district court. The window runs to 2021 because SCALES's judge field is the judge when the case was *collected* (2020–21), which can be a judge appointed after filing. (Rebuilt 24 Oct 2026; previously `fjc_district_judges_2016_17.csv`, covering 2016–17 only.) | FJC, *Biographical Directory of Article III Federal Judges* export, `judges.csv`, https://www.fjc.gov/history/judges/biographical-directory-article-iii-federal-judges-export | `senior_judge_at_filing` |
| `nchs_urban_rural_2013.csv` | One row per US county (FIPS code), with its 2013 NCHS urban–rural code (1–6) and Josh's grouping: **urban** = large central metro (1); **suburban** = large fringe metro (2); **rural** = medium metro, small metro, micropolitan, noncore (3–6). | CDC / NCHS, *2013 Urban–Rural Classification Scheme for Counties*, `NCHSURCodes2013.xlsx`, https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data/data_acces_files/NCHSURCodes2013.xlsx | `county_area_class` |
| `large_firms_bcg_2026.csv` | 188 large US law firms with attorney headcount, **rank by headcount** (183 have a headcount), the rank in buckets of 20, and the name prefixes used to match them | BCG Attorney Search, *Top U.S. Law Firms*, https://www.bcgsearch.com/sp/bcg-reports/law-firm-rankings/top-us-law-firms.php (compiled Aug 2026; FY2024–25 Am Law data). Only firm names and headcounts were kept. | `defendant_firm_rank` |

**Caveats**
- **The firm ranking is from 2024–25, not 2016–17.** Firms have grown, shrunk and merged since. Matching includes the 2016–17 names of firms that merged later (e.g. Troutman Sanders, Pepper Hamilton and Locke Lord → Troutman Pepper Locke; Allen & Overy and Shearman & Sterling → A&O Shearman), but sizes and ranks are as of 2024–25. Mention this in the write-up.
- **Firm matching is by name prefix** on cleaned names (lower case, no punctuation; "LLP", "PC" etc. ignored). A small firm whose name starts the same way as a listed firm could be mismatched. "Steptoe & Johnson PLLC" (a different West Virginia firm) is deliberately not matched to Steptoe LLP. Check the list of matched names in `checks/features_report_*.csv`.
- **The judge directory covers Article III judges only.** Magistrate judges aren't in it. `01_build_features.R` recognises them instead from each court's referral lists in SCALES (`referred_judges_json`) and "Magistrate Judge …" names. Territorial-court judges (Virgin Islands, Guam, Northern Mariana Islands) are labelled "territorial court". Judges sitting by designation in another court are matched from their home court (`judge_visiting`).
- **The 2013 county scheme** reflects 2012 population, the closest vintage to 2016–17. The FJC's county field is assumed to hold standard 5-digit FIPS codes. `01_build_features.R` reports the match rate so this can be checked.
